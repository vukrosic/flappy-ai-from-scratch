
=== step1_game.py ===
the world is 400 pixels high, the bird 24, the hole in a pipe 110; a pipe comes every 50 frames
a tap sets the speed to 8 pixels a frame upward; every frame without a tap adds 1 downward, up to 10
a game is scored up to 100 pipes; every player below plays the same 100 games

tapping at random
  a tap in   0% of the frames:  0.00 pipes a game, best game 0
  a tap in   5% of the frames:  0.05 pipes a game, best game 2
  a tap in  10% of the frames:  0.08 pipes a game, best game 1
  a tap in  20% of the frames:  0.02 pipes a game, best game 1
  a tap in  50% of the frames:  0.00 pipes a game, best game 0
  a tap in 100% of the frames:  0.00 pipes a game, best game 0

a rule written by a person: tap when the bird is more than N pixels below the middle of the hole
  N = -10:   2.11 pipes a game,   0 of 100 games without a mistake
  N =   0:  98.90 pipes a game,  98 of 100 games without a mistake
  N =  10: 100.00 pipes a game, 100 of 100 games without a mistake
  N =  20: 100.00 pipes a game, 100 of 100 games without a mistake
  N =  30: 100.00 pipes a game, 100 of 100 games without a mistake
  N =  40:   0.90 pipes a game,   0 of 100 games without a mistake

one game with N = 20 has 5000 frames, so 5000 decisions; the player is told "+1" 100 times
